About Me

Dat T. Tran (Tran Tan Dat)

My name is Dat T. Tran. I am currently a PhD student in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at UNSW Sydney, under the supervision of Professor Alina Ostafe and Professor Igor Shparlinski.

Prior to joining UNSW Sydney, I earned my Master's degree in Algebra and Number Theory from VNUHCM – University of Science (2022) and my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from Ho Chi Minh City University of Education (2019).
